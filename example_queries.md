## General

**Query:** `Explain what retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is and why it helps reduce hallucinations.`

**Answer:**

> Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a technique that enhances the output of large language models by integrating them with external knowledge bases, allowing for more accurate and reliable responses. This approach helps reduce hallucinations—instances where the model generates incorrect or nonsensical information—by grounding its answers in verified data sources [1][3].
>
> Key Points:
>
> * RAG optimizes AI model performance by connecting it to authoritative knowledge bases [2].
> * It improves accuracy and reduces hallucinations by grounding responses in real data [3].
> * The technique enhances the reliability of generative AI models through specific information retrieval [4].
>
